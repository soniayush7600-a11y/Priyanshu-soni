GET THE APP WITHOUT INSTALLING ANYTHING (build online with GitHub)
1. Make a free account at github.com and click New repository (name: upipayapp). Create it.
2. Click "uploading an existing file". Drag ALL files and folders from this UpiPayApp folder
   (including the hidden .github folder) into the page. Click Commit changes.
3. Click the "Actions" tab. Open "Build Android APK" and click "Run workflow" (or wait: it runs automatically).
4. After ~10 minutes it shows a green tick. Click the run, scroll to "Artifacts", download UpiPayApp-apk.
5. Unzip it, send the .apk file to your phone, tap it, and allow "Install unknown apps". Open "UPI Pay".
